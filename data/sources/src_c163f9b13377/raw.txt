Moreover, magnetic design for PS Portal. It is very convenient to charge. It only takes 3 hours to fully charge all devices. There are also charging indicators, with an orange light when charging and blue when fully charged.
*   【3 Levels Cooling Fan】The cooling system for PS5/PS5 Slim features 3 speeds cooling fan: High (4500RPM), Medium(3900RPM), Low(3500RPM). upgraded with a powerful turbo fan and promote better air circulation to reduce the temperature of your console effectively. All heated air will be blown out by the cooling stand at the bottom of the PS5 console and keep the system cool when playing games.

Long press the button for 2 seconds to turn on or off, short press for one second to switch the color.There are 5 modes of ambient light: red, green, blue, purple, and gradient lighting effects. Tips: The PS5 console stand has a memory function. The last LED mode will be maintained when turned on again.
*   【Space Saving & Stay Organized】The PS5/PS5 Slim/PS5 Pro horizontal stand minimizes the space taken up by the PS5 accessories, which can store the PS5/PS5 Slim/PS5 Pro console, PS Portal, remote control and controllers. With a extra USB Hub output port, and also has a hook to store the headphone, which keeps your PS5 console and accessories in order, reduce collisions, and better protect them.
*   【All in One Design】This PS5/PS5 Slim/PS5 Pro cooling stand comes with dual efficient cooling fan and 2 dual sense controller charging dock and P5 portal charging dock. What you will get: Charging & Cooling Stand X1, Charging Cable X1, Safety Lock Screw X1, Headphone Holder X 1, Magnetic Head X2, Adhesive Pad X1, Screws X 3, Bolts X 3, User ManualX1.
