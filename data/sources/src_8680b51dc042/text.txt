## David De Angelis

David De Angelis is a scholar and researcher of innovative physical and mental training techniques. He is the author of books that bring to light physiological techniques that allow you to achieve superior results in minimum time. The "PARETO EFFECTIVE" rule - of 80/20 - is a common note in all his books.

Among his best-known publications, there are "Power Flex Stretching - the secrets of super flexibility" and "The Secret of Perfect Vision: How You Can Prevent or Reverse Nearsightedness", currently translated into eight languages, in which he expounds Power Vision System, his innovative system of reduction and prevention of functional visual defects, such as myopia and hyperopia.

His books are characterized not only by the effectiveness of the techniques he expounds, but also by the fact that they are backed by scientific basis and supported by a solid scientific bibliography of reference.
