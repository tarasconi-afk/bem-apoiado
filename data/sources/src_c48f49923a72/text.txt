##### Quality issues

I wanted to love this comforter and spent a lot of time choosing it. It checked all the boxes for me - 80% goose down and/or fiber (the higher the feather content the lower quality the comforter), baffle stitching, 100% cotton shell, California king size, 750-800 fiber fill (this is determined only by the down content, not “feathers” which are heavy but not warm). The tag confirms the shell is cotton and at least 25% down 55% down fiber (pieces of down but not whole down fiber clusters) but it doesn’t specify it as being “goose”. If the tag doesn’t say “goose” specifically, it means that it’s filled with duck. Duck is not nearly as warm, soft or fluffy. This product, however, is advertised as being goose down.

It comes vacuum sealed to ship and takes at least 48 hours laid out to fluff up. In my case, I went through each baffled box and fluffed individually. I found there to be significant inconsistency in the filling across the boxes. In particular, I found the outer edges to be much fuller/fluffier than the ones across the middle. And sadly, there were a smattering of boxes that weren’t filled at all. The photo shows an example of 2 next to eachother that we’re nearly empty.

Essentially, a heavy blanket filled inconsistently like this, draped over a bed will flatten in the middle more so over time. A down comforter should last many years, this will definitely not.

This is the 2nd comforter I’ve bought, both were advertised as being 80% goose down. The first one was actually labeled as 75% “waterfowl” feather (crunchy duck feathers). Even still, given the quality issues and deceptive description, it’s going back to the seller. I’d rather spend more for a more quality product, know what is in it and that it will keep it’s shape. Sorry, this is a pass for me.
