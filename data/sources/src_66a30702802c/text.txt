##### After a week's use...

I wanted to try this blanket for a week of daily use before I gave it an adequate review.

I suffer from depression and anxiety, among other ailments, and I purchased a 4x6 15lb weighted blanket from a local retailer because of their known benefits. I'm a 5'11" male, and absolutely loathed that blanket. It was too much weight in such a focused space, so when I saw this queen size weighted blanket, I thought I'd give it a shot.

Pros:

- It feels like a blanket. Some weighted blankets feel like you're sleeping under burlap, or canvas. This blanket feels tremendously soft and is comfortable.

- I rated this 3/5 on the warmth scale. Do not be deterred by this. This is a complement. I run hot most nights, and I find this blanket doesn't overheat me. It will keep you warm on cooler nights, but you're not sweating buckets.

- The weight distribution is solid. It's to the point where I know there is weight on me, but it doesn't feel overly heavy.

- My dog loves it. Might as well ignore my points above and below for that reason alone.

Cons:

- I'm a little worried about the spot wash, industrial dryer/hang method of washing. Many weighted blankets are essentially a duvet and can be washed with ease. This one looks like it will require a little more diligence because washing of bedding is necessary in one's life.

- The beads in the blanket bunch up. This isn't a huge con, but you do notice the odd "tough" spot here and there.

Overall, I'm extraordinarily impressed with the blanket. For its price point, it brings nothing but value and is far superior to my $250+ weighted blanket I bought locally.
